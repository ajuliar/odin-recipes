Easy Homemade Tacos

Tacos are a versatile and delicious dish that can be customized to suit any taste. This easy tacos recipe will show you how to make flavorful tacos that are perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or a fun gathering with friends.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 small tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup chopped lettuce
  • 1/2 cup sliced jalapeños

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Add the taco seasoning and a little water (as directed on the seasoning packet) to the skillet. Stir to combine and let it simmer for a few minutes until the beef is fully coated with the seasoning.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave according to package instructions.
  4. Assemble the tacos by placing a portion of the seasoned beef onto each tortilla. Top with shredded cheese, diced tomatoes, chopped lettuce, and sliced jalapeños.
  5. Serve immediately and enjoy your delicious homemade tacos!
